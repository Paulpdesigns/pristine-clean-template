Built on consistency, not shortcuts

We started Pristine & Co. because too many cleaning visits felt rushed. Here's who we are and how we work.

Started in Manchester, built one careful visit at a time

Pristine & Co. opened in 2019 after our founder spent years managing cleaning crews for a larger regional company and kept seeing the same problem: rushed visits, inconsistent teams, and a checklist that existed on paper but not in practice.

We built Pristine & Co. around the opposite idea. The same small team returns to your property whenever possible, every visit follows a written checklist, and every cleaner is trained before they're sent out on their own. It's a slower way to grow a business, but it's the only way we've found to keep quality consistent.

What Drives Us

Our mission and values

A cleaning company should be judged the same way a client judges their own home: by the details.

Trust first

Every team member is background checked and every visit is insured, because access to your home or business is not something we take lightly.

Detail over speed

We'd rather run slightly longer on a visit than skip baseboards, grout lines, or the inside of an oven. The checklist exists for a reason.

Considerate by default

We default to low-toxicity, environmentally considerate products, and we're always happy to accommodate specific product requests or sensitivities.
